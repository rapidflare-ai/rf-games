    ABOUT RAPID GAMES

    BUILT BY RAPIDFLARE

    We are passionate about electronics. That is why we made these games: a playful way to make components, diagnostics, and problem solving part of the adventure.

    Who we are

    Rapidflare builds AI agents for technical sales and support in the electronics industry. Our team comes from Xilinx and the embedded world. We know how much useful knowledge lives in datasheets, catalogs, and application notes, so we build tools that help people find and use it.

    Rapid Games is a small side project from that same curiosity. Flare, our friendly game character, helps you work through a problem while you stay in control.

    How we think about AI

    AI can be helpful or harmful, depending on how people build and use it. There is plenty of gloom and doom around it. In our experience, when used well, AI can help people understand complex information and get useful work done.

    AI also uses real computing resources. We hope to keep reducing the resources needed to run it, and to work toward an environmental impact comparable to or lower than conventional computing for the same useful task. That is a goal, not a claim we have already achieved.
