    CIRCUIT SIEGE

    Frontier AI raised an army of ghouls. Flare is on our side.

    FLAREVS

    Choose a bay. Fit the part that fixes its fault. Launch to stop the ghouls.

    A just-for-fun side project. The ghouls are parodies and are not affiliated with any AI company.

    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Choose a bay. Spot its glowing fault shape.
    2. Match the shape in the part tray. Tap or drag to fit. The fighter needs two repairs.
    3. Launch the ready craft. The bay gets a new fault.

    Wrong fits cost 60 points and pause repairs for 1.6 seconds. Flare can auto-fit three parts per wave.

    One wave, every day. The daily wave is the same worldwide until midnight UTC. Practice runs are unlimited. No account needed.
