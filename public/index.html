    CIRCUIT SIEGE

    Frontier AI raised an army of ghouls. Flare is on our side.

    FLAREVS

    Flare, a friendly AI, scans each craft for faults. Read the diagnostic, choose the component that solves it, and launch from the shelter to stop the ghouls.

    A just-for-fun side project. The ghouls are parodies and are not affiliated with any AI company.

    HOW TO PLAY

    Pick a craft bay and read its fault. Tap a component or drag it directly onto the bay, then launch from the shelter. Selecting a bay does not change its fault. A correct fit advances the fighter to its second fault; launching gives the bay a new fault. Wrong fits cost points. Flare Auto-Fit solves one fault per use, three times per wave. Pause with the visible button to inspect the board; fitting or launching resumes play. Opening this panel also pauses the game.

    One wave, every day. The daily wave is the same worldwide until midnight UTC. Practice runs are unlimited. No account needed.
