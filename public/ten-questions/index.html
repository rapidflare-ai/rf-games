    TEN QUESTIONS
    TILL DAWN

    A ghoul is creeping toward the shelter. Flare knows which part will stop it.

    FLAREVS

    Ask Flare yes-or-no questions, cross out the parts that don't fit, and name the right one. Each question lets the ghoul creep one step closer; a wrong guess costs three. You have ten moves till dawn.

    A just-for-fun side project. The ghouls are parodies and are not affiliated with any AI company.

    HOW TO PLAY

    Flare has secretly picked one part. Each yes-or-no question moves the ghoul a step closer; a wrong guess moves it three. Read each part's spec line, cross out the ones that don't fit, and name the right one before the ghoul reaches the shelter.

    One case, every day. A hundred cases rotate through the year, the same worldwide until midnight UTC. Practice cases are unlimited. No account needed.
